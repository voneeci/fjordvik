# Fjordvik warranty policy

Document FJV-WP, Edition 3, January 2025.

> Synthetic document for a portfolio project. Fjordvik Manufacturing Oy is a fictional company; these instructions describe no real product.

## 1. Warranty period

Fjordvik equipment is under warranty for 24 months from installation, and at most 30 months from delivery, whichever ends first.

## 2. What is covered

Defects in materials or manufacture that appear in normal use within the period. Fjordvik repairs or replaces the faulty part, and pays the labour and travel for the repair.

## 3. What is not covered

- Wear parts (mechanical seals, bearings, impellers, wear rings, gaskets, filter mats), unless they fail within 6 months of installation
- Damage from dry running, cavitation caused by poor suction conditions, or clogging by foreign objects
- Damage from parts that are not genuine Fjordvik parts
- Equipment under a service contract whose preventive visits were refused

## 4. Making a claim

Claim within 30 days of finding the fault, with the serial number, a description and photographs. Keep the faulty part until Fjordvik decides the claim.

## 5. Parts replaced under warranty

A part replaced under warranty is covered for 12 months, or for the rest of the original period if that is longer.

## 6. Decisions

Fjordvik's After-sales Service decides claims within 10 business days of receiving everything listed in section 4.
