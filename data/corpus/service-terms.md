# Fjordvik service terms

Document FJV-ST, Edition 3, January 2025.

> Synthetic document for a portfolio project. Fjordvik Manufacturing Oy is a fictional company; these instructions describe no real product.

## 1. Scope

These terms apply to service work on Fjordvik pumps, panels and drives in Finland, Sweden, Estonia and Latvia. Elsewhere, Fjordvik's distributors serve customers on their own terms.

## 2. Service contracts

| Contract | Preventive visits a year | Remote monitoring | Response to a breakdown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| None | 0 | No | Best effort, quoted per job |
| Basic | 1 | Readings stored, reviewed at visits | Within 3 business days |
| Premium | 2 | Readings stored, reviewed at visits | Technician on site within 24 hours on business days |

### 2.1 What a preventive visit includes

Labour, travel and the service kit for each covered unit. Repairs found during the visit are quoted separately.

### 2.2 Changing contract

A customer can move up a level at any time and down a level at the yearly renewal.

## 3. Parts

### 3.1 Genuine parts

Fjordvik fits genuine parts only. Prices follow the customer's parts price list.

### 3.2 Delivery

Parts in stock ship the same business day from Oulu Warehouse or Tartu Warehouse. Parts not in stock take the lead time in the manual's parts list.

### 3.3 Returns

Unused parts in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## 4. Readings from connected pumps

Pumps with a Fjordvik drive send daily readings to Fjordvik. The customer owns the readings; Fjordvik uses them to plan service and improve its products, and shares them with no one else without the customer's consent.

## 5. Work orders and approval

Every job has a work order with a unique number. Work beyond the agreed scope needs the customer's approval first, except to make the site safe.
