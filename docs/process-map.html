Fjordvik Reporting Process Map

How one month's emissions travel from a site's boilers, meters and invoices to the Power BI report: every step, the job title that owns it, the tool it uses and the data it needs. The map shows the process as Fjordvik would run it, and marks what this project has built, what is designed and tested offline, and what sits outside the project.

Fjordvik Manufacturing Oy is a fictional company and this is a portfolio project on synthetic data. Job titles are roles, not people. Emission factors are illustrative, not cited, and must not be used for real reporting.

Tap a status to highlight its steps. Tap a step for its tool, owner and data.
